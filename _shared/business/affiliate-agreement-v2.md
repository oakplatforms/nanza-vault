## Title

Nanza Affiliate Link — Our Understanding

## Body

This is the understanding between you and Nanza (Oak Platforms, Inc.) for your affiliate link. It is not a legal contract. It is a plain record of what we are offering, what we are asking, and how the money works, so neither of us has to remember it from a call. If anything here surprises you, tell us before you tap agree.

What you get

Your affiliate link is a shareable URL for referrals. When someone downloads Nanza through it and signs up, they are counted as your referral and you earn as they use Nanza.

How the link pays

Each referred user can earn you up to $20, in four $5 tiers. The tiers are independent, each one pays once per referred user, and there is no time limit on reaching them.

1. Five posts: the user creates five posts or replies, at any point in their time on Nanza.

2. Five offers: the user sends five offers to five different people. Nothing has to be accepted.

3. Five listings: the user becomes a seller and creates five listings. Nothing has to sell.

4. Ten collection cards: the user adds ten cards to their collection, by using search or by scanning.

How you get paid

Each tier a referred user reaches creates a voucher on your account, which we review and approve as admins. Approved vouchers are paid to you through your Nanza seller account on Stripe. We review on a rolling basis and aim to pay within a day of approval. You can see what you have earned by asking us; an in-app view is on the roadmap.

What we ask of you

Share your link with your audience on your social channels, in your own voice. Tell us what works and what does not, honestly.

Ground rules

A referral counts when someone new installs Nanza through your link and signs up with a new account. Referring yourself, your own alternate accounts, or accounts created only to hit a tier does not count, and we reserve the right to decline vouchers that look that way. Attribution is first touch: if a user arrived through another affiliate first, they stay with that affiliate.

Beyond the link

The affiliate link is one part of the Nanza affiliate program. Vendor referral commission, premium content, and brand sponsorships are separate arrangements with their own terms, and nothing here commits either of us to them.

What this is not

This is not an exclusive arrangement, an employment relationship, or a guarantee of income. Either of us can step away at any time; vouchers already approved are still paid.

If the terms change

Once you have accepted this, its text does not change. If the affiliate link changes in a way that matters, we will write a new version and ask you to accept it before it applies to you. Until then, this is the deal, and we are glad you are a part of our journey.
