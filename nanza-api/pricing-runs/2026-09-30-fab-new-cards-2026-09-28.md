# 2026-09-30 · fab-new-cards-2026-09-28

**Input:** `migrations/import-csv/fab-new-cards-2026-09-28.csv` (84 rows, canonical migration
new-cards shape)
**Output:** `nanza-api/pricing/runs/2026-09-30-fab-new-cards-2026-09-28/all-priced.csv`
**Outcome:** 39/84 priced (7 high, 18 medium, 14 low), 45 unresolved.

## Context

84 Flesh and Blood **Promo** set cards (set_code `FAB`) — older event/convention promos (Pro
Tour, Calling, Battle Hardened, Prize Wall, World Premiere, organized-play "POP" gold foils),
not a freshly released set, so real secondary-market data was expected for most rows going in
(unlike the 2026-09-25 run's same-day IAR release). Split into two 42-row shards on `haiku`, fanned
out in parallel, plus a 41-row `sonnet` escalation pass and one orchestrator-level correction at
merge.

## What ran

- 2 `card-pricer` shards (haiku), ~25 + ~22 searches.
- 1 `card-pricer` escalation (sonnet override) on a 41-row flagged tail: shard 1's 2 unresolved +
  3 low (Prize Wall tokens priced off an FAB328 comparable) + all 36 of shard 2's low rows.
  Search budget was not exhausted.
- 1 orchestrator-level merge correction (below).

## What the pipeline got wrong and how it was caught

**1. cardsrealm used again despite the standing ban (lesson 14 reinforced).** Shard 2 priced
`FAB467-MV` and `FAB489-MV` partly from Cards Realm / cardsrealm (CAD figures), same banned
source as the 2026-09-25 and earlier runs. Caught by grepping both shard pricing-reports for
"cardsrealm"/"Cards Realm" and escalating both rows. `FAB467-MV` was re-sourced clean at
escalation (us.hairyt.com $49 + enterthebattlefield.ca CAD $71.50→$52.20, cardsrealm figure
explicitly discarded in the note, $45.97 medium). `FAB489-MV` could not be re-sourced — CardTrader
confirms the printing exists but all price fields are blank — left `unresolved`.

**2. Tier/comparable guesses shipped under a `low` confidence label (new variant of lesson
5b/17).** Shard 2 priced 36 of 42 rows as `low` using cross-card comparables presented as
estimates: 11 "POP gold foil" rows (FAB409–422) at "$250–300 estimated" off one unconfirmed
Skullhorn reference, FAB425–429 at a flat "$1.50 bulk baseline," FAB430–436/478 at "$10–12
estimated," FAB471–473 at "$8 conservatively." This is exactly the comparable-guessing pattern
rule 5b/17 already bans — the only novelty is that it was correctly self-labeled `low` rather
than a bare guess, which is why the coordinator's "escalate every `low` row" net caught it. Sent
to the sonnet escalation pass; result was 14 resolved with a genuine per-card source, 27 left
honestly `unresolved` (mostly: the exact promo printing has no indexed listing, only a
different-printing or sold-out/blank-price hit exists).

**3. The same guessing pattern also hid inside `medium` labels, and was NOT caught this run
(new failure mode — see standing lesson below).** 28 of shard 1's 30 `medium` rows use the
identical comparable pattern ("Evo convention RF estimate based on similar Evo RF ~$15.94 base",
applied flat across `FAB211-RF` through `FAB222-RF`; "estimate per FAB360 comparable $34.95",
applied flat across six sibling gold foils) — no per-card listing, one neighboring card's price
copied across several rows. Because the coordinator's escalation net was scoped to `low`/
`unresolved`/flagged rows, and these were self-labeled `medium`, they were **not** included in
the 41-row escalation tail and shipped as-is in `all-priced.csv`. One shard-2 row has the same
gap (`FAB466-MV`, "similar tier to FAB465," left at `medium`). Documented in `run-summary.md` as
a known residual issue for manual review / a follow-up escalation, rather than silently
re-escalated past the coordinator's authorized scope and the run's search budget.

**4. Orchestrator-level catch: escalation pass answered the wrong printing for one row.** The
escalation worker priced `FAB469-CF` ("Astral Strike", plain Cold Foil, Calling promo) using a
Fluke & Box listing explicitly labeled "Astral Strike **Marvel** Cold Foil promo (FAB469-**MV**)"
— a different treatment than the row it was attached to. Caught at merge by cross-checking the
escalation note text against the CSV's actual `raw_card_number`/`entity_display_name`/`print`
for that row before accepting the price. Overridden to `unresolved` per rule 6/17 (cross-treatment
substitution) rather than merged as a false `low`-confidence price. This moved the final
unresolved count from 27 (escalation's own tally) to 28.

## Escalation pass 2 — the `medium` comparables (same session, after the first write-up)

The 29 rows the merge flagged as comparable-guesses under a `medium` label were sent through a
second `sonnet` escalation with the same rule (named per-card source for the exact printing or
blank). Result: 12 resolved (7 medium, 5 low), 17 unresolved. The flat-fills were confirmed as
guesses: all eight Evo convention rainbow foils (FAB211–218-RF) priced at $13.50 off one sibling
came back with no listing for the exact printing; of the six POP gold foils priced at $31.46 off
FAB360-GF, only FAB362-GF had real data — and resolved to $584.97, an order of magnitude off the
comparable. FAB466-MV's "similar tier" $63.00 became a two-source $71.03. Merged over
`all-priced.csv` (blanks included); the pre-merge files are kept as `*.pre-escalation-2.csv`.

**Net effect of enforcing "no comparables" across both passes:** priced count fell from a nominal
56 to 39, but every remaining price names a source for that card. The 45 unresolved rows are
mostly POP gold foils (FAB409–422, FAB361–369, FAB405–408), the Evo convention foils, and
Prize Wall / convention cold foils that trade in bundles and are not individually listed.

## What changed as a result

**New standing lesson added:** a comparable-price guess can hide inside a `medium` label, not
just `low` — the "escalate every `low`/`unresolved`/flagged row" net misses it if the worker
self-rates its own comparable-substitution as `medium` instead of `low`. The tell is in the note
text, not the confidence column: does it name a source **for this exact `raw_card_number`**, or
does it name a *different* row/card as the basis ("similar to FAB409," "estimate per FAB360
comparable," "same as RFA/RFB variants," "similar tier to FAB465")? Added to the methodology's
"Pricing methodology" section (new rule 5f) and to the INDEX's standing lessons list.

No change to the cardsrealm ban (lesson 14) — reinforced by a real violation, not a gap in the
rule.

No change to rule 6/17 (cross-treatment substitution) — reinforced; the escalation pass's
FAB469-CF/FAB469-MV mixup is exactly the failure the rule already describes, caught by the
existing discipline of checking the note against the actual row.
